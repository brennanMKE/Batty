## Description

Write the **Notifications** section of Batty's user Help: `Batty/Help/06-notifications.md`. Covers how Batty signals you when something happens in a non-focused tab — the chip dot, the sidebar badge, desktop notifications, and the bell feed.

Depends on `#0080` (Help window bootstrap).

## What `06-notifications.md` covers

### What counts as a "bell"

- **Plain `\a` BEL** — any process emitting the ASCII bell character. The shell at a prompt rings the bell with `printf '\a'`; many TUIs ring it on completion or alerts.
- **Desktop notifications via OSC 9** — terminal apps can emit a richer notification with a title + body (the iTerm2 / rxvt-unicode convention). When supported by the app you're running, you get a real macOS notification.

### Where bells appear

When a bell rings in a tab you're not currently looking at:

1. **A dot appears on the tab's chip** — small accent-colored circle indicating "something happened here."
2. **A badge appears on the session** in the sidebar — shows how many unseen bells across all tabs in the session.
3. **A desktop notification fires** (if enabled in Settings, and the session isn't muted).
4. **The bell feed records the event** — `Cmd-Shift-N` to open the popover and see the timestamp, the session/pane/tab, and an optional message body.

If the bell rings in the tab you're already looking at, only the bell feed records it. No badge, no notification — you saw it.

### Clearing notifications

Bells clear automatically when you visit the tab they rang in. Switching to that tab — by clicking, hotkey, geometric arrow movement, or chip click — marks every unseen bell from that tab as seen and decrements the session/pane badge accordingly.

Manual clear: from the menu, **Window → Mark All Bells Seen** clears every unseen count and marks every feed entry as seen.

### The Bell Feed popover

- `Cmd-Shift-N` toggles. The toolbar bell icon also opens it.
- Lists every recorded bell, newest first.
- Click an entry to jump straight to its tab. Selecting and focus follow automatically.
- Entries for closed tabs / panes / sessions disappear automatically (per `#0071`) — you'll never see a feed entry pointing at something that's gone.

### Muting a session

Right-click a session row in the sidebar → **Mute Notifications**. While muted:

- Desktop notifications for the session are suppressed.
- The chip dot and sidebar badge still update — mute affects delivery, not capture.
- Bell sound (libghostty's audible bell) is **not** suppressed in v1 — only the visual delivery.

Right-click again → **Unmute Notifications** to restore.

### Settings → Notifications

- **Play sound** — toggle the audible bell.
- **Show system notifications** — global toggle for the macOS-level notification banner.
- Help row: per-session mute lives on the sidebar's right-click menu.

If desktop notifications don't appear even though both toggles are on, check **System Settings → Notifications → Batty** — macOS has its own permission gate.
