## Description

`PRD.md:257` still says: "A 'Clear all' button marks every entry seen." #0638 replaced that button with **Mark All Read** and **Clear Read** in the Bell Feed's header, and added **Window ▸ Mark All Bells Read** and **Window ▸ Clear Read Bells** (`issues/0638.md`, `## Fix`). `Concepts.md` (version 0.34, the **Unread and read** subsection at `:270`), `BattyKit/Sources/BattyKit/Help/06-notifications.md:25-31` and `docs/notifications.md` were updated by #0638. `PRD.md` was not.

## Expected behavior

`PRD.md`'s Bell Feed section (around `:244-258`) describes the shipped behaviour in the `Concepts.md` terms: rows are **unread** or **read**; **Mark All Read** (header) and **Window ▸ Mark All Bells Read** mark every row read and keep it; **Clear Read** (header) and **Window ▸ Clear Read Bells** remove read rows and leave unread ones; a row click brings the source forward and removes that Tab's rows (#0556). Point to `Concepts.md` "Unread and read" rather than restating the rules.
