1 · Where do already-open Projects go?
Today they sink to the end, so the big cards show Projects you haven't opened. Alternatives: keep their rank, or a separate "Open" strip (Option C). Recommend: sink.
2 · How many big cards, and how big?
A: 3 at 248 pt wide · B: 2 at 380 pt · C: 4 at 184 pt. The sheet grows from 720×520 to 860×600. Recommend: A.
3 · Fewer Projects than the top row holds?
With up to 3 Projects, everything is a big card and there's no second row. Recommend: as drawn in "Two Projects, and none".
4 · While filtering?
Typing collapses to one compact grid ranked by match, so the best match is first and Return opens it. Recommend: collapse.
5 · Arrow keys across the two rows
↓ from a big card goes to the nearest small card below; ↑ from the first small row returns to the big card above. Home/End jump to first/last. Recommend: as described.
6 · Empty state and a "+ New Project" card
Keep today's empty state with a New Project… button, and no "+" card in the grid (⌘N is in the footer). Recommend: no "+" card.
7 · "Opened 2 h ago" on cards
Shown on big cards only (A, B, C); small cards keep path and summary. Recommend: big cards only.
8 · Recency only, or also how often?
Recency is simple and predictable. Counting opens would need a new stored field. Recommend: recency only for 1.3.0.
