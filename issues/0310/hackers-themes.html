Hackers — four candidate themes, dark and light

Drawn from the quad poster (magenta/violet, acid-yellow wordmark), the teaser poster (cold blue monochrome), and the Gibson mainframe visualization. Each card shows a real terminal mock, the 16 ANSI slots, and a ready-to-paste GhosttyThemeDefinition. Contrast is foreground-on-background, WCAG ratio.

Batty stores the global theme per appearance — co.sstools.Batty.themeName.dark and co.sstools.Batty.themeName.light — and re-applies on system appearance change, so each theme needs a companion or Light-mode users fall back to TokyoNight Day. The companions follow that theme's slot convention: slot 0 is a near-background tint, slot 15 is the ink, and slots 9–14 repeat 1–6 rather than brightening, because "brighter" on paper means less legible.
