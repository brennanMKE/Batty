## Description

#0361 split the CLI's "broker reports no endpoint" case into two arms:
`.launchAndPoll` (nothing running — start the app, poll until it registers) and
`.waitForReregistration` (the app *is* running at the resolved bundle path but has
not registered — wait for it rather than launching a duplicate). Both arms share
`pollForEndpoint` with the same defaults, so the second arm inherits a bound
sized for a cold app launch.

Those defaults are `pollMaxAttempts: 20`, `pollInterval: 0.25`,
`pollTimeout: 1.0`. As `AppConnectDance.resolveEndpoint`'s own doc comment works
out, the worst case against a broker that accepts connections but never replies
is `20 × 1.0 + 19 × 0.25 ≈ 24.8 s` — not the ~5 s that `maxAttempts × interval`
alone suggests. So a user who types `batty status` while the app is up but
unregistered, against a wedged broker, waits roughly 25 seconds for exit `6`.

The bound is doing its job — the CLI exits rather than hanging — but 25 seconds
of silence is the wrong shape for this specific arm. The `.launchAndPoll` arm is
waiting on a cold `NSApplication` launch and deserves headroom; the
`.waitForReregistration` arm is waiting on an already-running process to notice a
broker, which either happens quickly or is not going to happen at all.

The #0361 implementer flagged this in that issue's `## Gotchas` rather than
fixing it, to keep round scope tight.

## Expected behavior

`batty <verb>` against a running-but-unregistered app fails fast enough to feel
like an answer — a few seconds — with a message that says what to do (check or
relaunch Batty), rather than appearing hung for ~25 s.

## Actual behavior

Up to ~24.8 s of no output, then exit `6`.

## Notes

### Shape of the fix

Close to a one-liner: `resolveEndpoint` already threads `pollMaxAttempts`,
`pollInterval`, and `pollTimeout` down to `pollForEndpoint`, so the
`.waitForReregistration` arm can pass its own tighter values without touching the
`.launchAndPoll` arm.

Things to decide rather than assume:

- What "fast enough" is. There is no measured number for how long a live app
  takes to re-register after a broker restart, so picking a bound is a judgment
  call — say so in the resolution rather than presenting it as derived.
- Whether to shorten `pollTimeout` (the per-attempt round trip, which is what
  actually dominates the 24.8 s) or `pollMaxAttempts`, or both. Shortening
  `pollTimeout` alone changes behavior against a *slow* broker, not just a wedged
  one.
- Whether the same reasoning applies to `.launchAndPoll`. It probably does not —
  cold launch genuinely can take seconds — but the current sharing of one bound
  between two very different waits is the underlying smell.
- The user-facing message on exit `6` matters at least as much as the duration.
  Verify what the CLI actually prints today before tuning only the timing.

Note the seams named here are exactly the ones **#0365** reports as untestable
from `BattyKitTests` — a test asserting "the re-registration arm uses the shorter
bound" cannot be written until that is resolved. Worth sequencing #0365 first if
both are being worked.

## Resolution notes

> 🟢 Resolved 2026-09-23 — the `.waitForReregistration` arm has its own poll bound, sized to catch the running app's first 5 s broker-reconnect backoff retry, with a worst case of about 12.5 s instead of ~24.8 s.

## Root cause

Both no-endpoint arms shared `pollMaxAttempts: 20`, `pollTimeout: 1.0`, `pollInterval: 0.25` — a bound sized for a cold `NSApplication` launch.

## Fix

- `AppConnectDance.resolveEndpoint` gained `reregistrationPollMaxAttempts = 13`, `reregistrationPollInterval = 0.5`, `reregistrationPollTimeout = 0.5`, used only by `.waitForReregistration`. `.launchAndPoll` is unchanged.
- Sizing: `AppXPCCoordinator` re-registers immediately while its `BrokerReconnectBudget` (5) lasts, then retries on `BrokerReconnectBackoff` (5, 10, 20, 40, capped 60 s). The last attempt lands at 12 × 0.5 = 6.0 s, 1 s past the first backoff retry; later gaps, and an app whose `SMAppService` is not `.enabled` (no retry scheduled), are what exit 6 is for.
- Worst case against a broker that wedges during the wait: 13 × 0.5 + 12 × 0.5 = 12.5 s. Each attempt opens a fresh `NSXPCConnection` with signing checks, so the per-attempt timeout stays at 0.5 s; a timed-out attempt is retried, not fatal.
- Docs: exit-6 timing noted in `docs/batty-cli-agent-workflows.md`, `docs/batty-cli-install.md`, and the Batty-deviation block of `docs/xpc/xpc-cli-architecture.md`.

## Review

Reviewer `claude-opus-5-5` (Issues.md pins `claude-opus-4-8`; the Agent tool's `opus` alias resolves to 5.5), three rounds. Round 1: the doc comment ignored `BrokerReconnectBackoff` and the shortened healthy-broker window (the first version's 8 attempts × 0.25 s gave only ~1.75 s); the orchestrator asked for a decision on covering the first backoff retry. Round 2: the chosen 0.1 s per-attempt timeout was too tight for a fresh connection under load and its justification leaned on a vendored figure; back to 0.5 s. Round 3 approved.

- The 0.5 s re-registration timeout is a literal, not derived from `pollTimeout`; changing one doesn't move the other.
- Neither worst-case figure includes the initial 3 s `brokerTimeout` fetch that runs before either poll.
