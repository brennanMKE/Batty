## Description

This is the reap-registry child of umbrella [#0427](0427.md), and it is **blocked by [#0428](0428.md)**. #0427 counts 4 sites in `PendingTeardownReapRegistryTests.swift`. #0428 takes three of them as the proving use of its pipe readiness helper: the unbounded `waitForReady(_ pipe:)` copy at `:44-46` and its calls at `:90` and `:119`. #0428 also answers the question the last site depends on. What remains here is the `kill(pid, 0)` poll after `drainSynchronously()`. `drainSynchronously()` reaps before it returns, so the loop is at best redundant. At worst it is hiding a zombie-vs-exited ambiguity that should be pinned down, not slept past.
