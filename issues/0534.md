## Fix

Both lines now state no count, so the next added symbol cannot make them stale:

- `SFSymbolNames.txt:19`: `# curated Project symbols (ProjectSymbol) are added at load whether or not they appear here.` Comment only; `SFSymbolCatalog` skips `#` lines.

The real count stays pinned by `ProjectSymbolTests` and `ProjectAppearanceTests` and by the `ProjectSymbol.swift` doc comment.
