### Customizing shortcuts

- Open **Settings → Shortcuts** (`Cmd-,`).
- Click a recorder, press the new combo. Press `Esc` to cancel.
- Each row has a Reset button; the pane bottom has Reset All.
- Warnings appear when two actions share the same combo, or when the combo matches a macOS-reserved one (`Cmd-Q`, `Cmd-H`, `Cmd-M`, `Cmd-Tab`, `Cmd-,`). Reserved combos still save with the warning visible.
- Customizations persist across launches.

### Fixed (non-customizable) shortcuts

`Cmd-1` through `Cmd-9` (tab selection) and `Cmd-Option-1` through `Cmd-Option-9` (session selection) are positional and intentionally not customizable. `Cmd-C` / `Cmd-V` follow the system clipboard convention.

### Why some shortcuts work everywhere

Batty's shortcuts fire **regardless of which view has keyboard focus** — the terminal, the sidebar, the tab strip, or a popover. They go through an app-level event monitor rather than the menu bar's standard handling. This means you can press `Cmd-N` from inside a terminal session and it creates a new session, not sending Cmd-N to the shell.

### When Help can't override

A handful of macOS system shortcuts can't be overridden by any app, including Batty: Mission Control, Spotlight, screenshot capture, and screen-recording trigger combinations. These behave normally regardless of Batty's settings.
